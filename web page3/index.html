Introduction

UX design focuses on the overall experience, while UI design emphasizes how a product looks and operates.

UI design ("User Interface Design") covers elements like icons, buttons, typography, color schemes, and responsive design. It enhances the user's interaction with digital devices or products, ensuring both aesthetics and functionality.

UX design ("User Experience Design") takes a human-first approach to designing products. It ensures users can accomplish tasks effortlessly while feeling satisfied with their overall experience.
